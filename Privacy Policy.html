Privacy Policy — Dark PDF Viewer

Last updated: October 6, 2026

What this extension does

Dark PDF Viewer is a browser extension that opens PDF documents in its own built-in viewer, offering a light/dark toggle, text selection, search, and a contact form.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • PDF documents. PDFs you open are fetched directly from the website hosting them (or read from your local disk) and rendered locally in your browser. They are never uploaded to any server operated by the developer.
  • Browsing activity. The extension observes navigation events only to detect when a page is a PDF document. It does not record, store, or transmit your browsing history.
  • Settings. Your viewing preferences (dark/light mode, zoom level) are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never synced or transmitted.

Contact form

The optional "Contact Deveoper" form in the viewer submits the name, email address, and message you type to Formspree, a third-party form service, which forwards it to the developer's email address. Formspree processes this submission under its own privacy policy. No other data — including which PDF you are viewing — is sent with the form.

Permissions

  • webNavigation — used solely to detect top-level navigations to PDF files so they can be opened in the built-in viewer.
  • Host permissions — used solely to fetch the PDF file you choose to open. The extension does not read, modify, or transmit the content of any webpage.

Third-party services

  • Formspree (contact form delivery only)
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with any third party.
